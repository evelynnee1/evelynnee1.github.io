WHO AM I?

NAJLA QAILA NUR AZKHASA (Call me Nana)

Hi! I’m Nana, and this is where my creations live. I am a professional Graphic Designer and Illustrator who is eager for more ways to express myself. I draw, I read, and I tune out the background noise. Welcome to my digital archive, fueled by ethereal synths, dark aesthetics, and medieval literature.

I have loved design and drawing since childhood, holding a deep interest in both graphic design and digital illustration. I enjoy exploring various design styles and studying the meaning and philosophy behind every piece of design and art. Look around, or don't. I don't really mind.
